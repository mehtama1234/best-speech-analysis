## Candidate patterns and next checks

Candidates are early announcement of a thesis followed by its rationale;
frustration reframed as a resource; stable-purpose/changed-means narrative;
and a reordered closing summary with an opening-frame callback. These need
comparisons and counterexamples, not automatic promotion into best practices.

A provisional exercise is to state a purpose, explain why one attempted route
changed, name enabling resources and uncertainty, and close by returning to the
audience's original problem. Test comprehension and perceived relevance rather
than assuming a famous speaker's career establishes effectiveness.
