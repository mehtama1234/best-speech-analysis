Compound speech-pattern practice

These bundles pair recurring candidate moves found together in the full-context corpus. Co-occurrence is a discovery lead, not proof that the moves work better together or belong in this order.

bundle-audience_invitation_and_next_turn-then-objection_and_response

Co-occurrence: 6 reviews / 3 videos

Boundary: Co-occurrence is a discovery signal, not evidence that the two moves work better together or belong in this order.
