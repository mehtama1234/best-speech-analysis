# 30-day route through the full speech corpus

Status: **every reviewed video scheduled for inspection and practice; not validation evidence**.

This route schedules inspection and practice of every reviewed video. It is not validation evidence and does not rank videos, establish effectiveness, or replace independent review and listener/learner testing.

Videos: **395** · days: **29** · maximum videos in one day: **14**.

## Daily loop

1. Read the exact evidence for two listed videos and open the linked writeup.
2. Name the listener problem and the observed move before looking at the phrase handle.
3. Rewrite one repeatable form in your own relationship, evidence level and access mode.
4. Try it once; ask for a listener restatement when possible.
5. Record the non-fit, safety/accessibility issue and next revision in the local practice log.

## Route

The route deliberately rotates canonical jobs so the month does not become a single-speaker or single-style imitation exercise.
