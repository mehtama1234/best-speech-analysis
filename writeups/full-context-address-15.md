## 2. Belonging does not promise a flawless performance

characters then illustrates the idea that difference can have a place.

## 3. The leap analogy distinguishes progress from exploitation

The character's physical world provides the analogy; the social proposition
concerns collaboration. It is not evidence that a real childhood episode occurred,
nor does the story demonstrate that cooperation always produces better outcomes.

An eye-closing joke likewise draws on persona rather than supplying independently

## 4. Connection expands beyond networking

The third section begins with jobs, professional contacts and material concerns.
It then shifts to family perspectives, supporters who may be absent, and staying

Those meanings should remain separate. Summarizing the section as a networking

be okay is not a verified forecast of their circumstances.

The inclusive reference to family or special supporters is broader than assuming
that every graduate has the same parental relationship. Even so, individual
families' feelings and audience reactions have not been independently observed.
