**Practice hypothesis:** state the question or requested disclosure plainly

**Boundary:** this scene uses pressure as drama; it is not a model for consent.

**Communication job:** show a character opening up by adding one remembered

**Risk:** a list can turn disclosure into spectacle or invite an audience to
