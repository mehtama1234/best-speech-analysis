## Candidate patterns and next checks

Candidates include expanding a decision's criteria; partial agreement before
redefining a metric; moving an identity question to shared responsibilities; and
answering a forecast request with an explicit uncertainty statement plus a method.
Compare these with evasive answers and unsupported assurances, not only similar
success stories. Preserve family, capital, institutional and platform access as
possible confounds.

A provisional exercise is to distinguish what you cannot predict from what a
listener can practice, then state the resources and limits that practice assumes.
Test whether listeners can recover both parts. This is not an admitted best-
practice card, a cognitive-health conclusion or a validated AI-proof career plan.
