## Research implications

Candidate structures include opposite outcomes converging on the same next step;
a broad claim qualified by an exception; an exaggerated claim repaired by a
follow-up; and persistence combined with a change of method. These were already
visible in the compilation. This reading strengthens contextual documentation
and source reconciliation, not independent recurrence counts.

A provisional exercise is to explain what stayed constant after a setback and
what changed, keeping uncertainty, resources and exceptions explicit. Its value
needs testing; no practice card is admitted here. Compare distinct original
events and cases where revision did not succeed before claiming a general rule.

Next carry the dependency into downstream synthesis, authenticate original media
where needed and inspect continuous video before claims about demonstration,
gesture or audience response. Transcript repetition is not measured vocal
emphasis, and host praise is not independent effectiveness evidence.
