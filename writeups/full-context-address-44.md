## Candidate pattern and its limits

The candidate is a personal story whose relationship structure is transferred to
the audience's shared responsibility, with a recurring label linking the two.
The scope caveats are part of that structure: complexity of identity, imperfect
institutions, imperfect practitioners and responsibility not resting on one person.
