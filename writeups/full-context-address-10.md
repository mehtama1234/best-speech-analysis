## 5. Several ideals become proposed decision rules

Fairness receives a proposed practice: hear another side and use data. Openness
is connected to hiring capable people and permitting questions. Transparency and
conscience culminate in a short disclosure-under-doubt rule.

These moves are more specific than naming virtues alone. They identify behavior
that could be examined in practice. However, the source does not establish that
each rule is sufficient, universally appropriate or followed consistently.
Business, pricing and cash-flow maxims remain attributed advice rather than
validated financial principles.

## 6. The conclusion expands the scope beyond the original case

The company began as a bounded experiment in the narrative. The ending presents
the principles as timeless and applicable across contexts, then asks graduates
to internalize and transmit them to future generations.

That scope expansion is analytically important. A personal organizational account
does not establish universality merely because the closing asserts it. The
windshield-fog image illustrates hoped-for guidance under uncertainty; it is
not measured decision improvement.

The final originality appeal belongs with the preceding values framework, not
as permission to ignore evidence or obligations.
