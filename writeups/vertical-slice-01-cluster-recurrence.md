# Vertical slice 01: cluster recurrence

This report counts the five pilot clusters at the packet-selection level. It
does not prove that the labels are correct, that the events are independent or
that a form improves audience outcomes.

| Cluster | Representative occurrences | Unique video IDs | Representatives with raw audio/video | Counterexamples |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Contrast/disagreement | 5 | 4 | 5 | 0 |
| Example | 5 | 3 | 5 | 6 |
| Story/personal experience | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Uncertainty/qualification | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| Conclusion/summary | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |

Across the five packets there are 25 representative occurrences spanning 13 unique canonical video IDs; all packet roles together span 16 IDs. The occurrence counts are not independent-event counts: some source IDs recur across packets, and the source/event audit does not yet verify speaker identity or event independence. The story packet includes the one source whose upload is unavailable, so its raw-media denominator is four of five.

The report therefore supports a useful next question—whether these five
higher-level jobs recur across speakers and settings—but not the conclusion
that they are proven universal best practices.
