## How to use this inventory

- Open both source clips for a job and compare the transcript function with what is actually visible and audible.
- Record uncertainty when the speaker, turn, edit or caption alignment is unclear.
- Treat the numbers as navigation aids and the midpoint notes as bounded observations, not as instructions to imitate.
- Promotion requires the independent delivery, counterexample, learner and listener gates in the [end-to-end goal](end-to-end-goal.md).
