Vertical slice 02 learner check

Research boundary: This records teachability observations for three unvalidated candidate patterns. It does not test trust, memory, persuasion, engagement or action. Do not enter identifying personal stories or sensitive information. Written, typed, captioned and asynchronous responses are allowed.
Session record
1. candidate-question
recognition — question-recognition-01

Stimulus: I wonder what I could try next?

Identify whether this is information-seeking, reflection, a rhetorical move or another function. Explain what evidence you used.

transfer — question-transfer-01

Stimulus: A colleague gives you a complicated explanation.

Write one respectful question that invites clarification without implying the colleague failed.

non_fit — question-nonfit-01

Stimulus: You already know the answer and want the group to agree.

Explain when a rhetorical question could pressure the group and write a bounded alternative.

control — question-control-01

Stimulus: You need to move directly from one agenda item to the next.

Write a plain transition without a question, then explain why a question is not necessary here.

2. candidate-definition_or_reformulation
recognition — definition-recognition-01

Stimulus: By ‘access,’ I mean the ability to use the service without an extra barrier.

Identify whether this is a definition, translation, interpretation or recommendation, and name the limit it states or leaves open.

transfer — definition-transfer-01

Stimulus: Your audience does not know a technical term.

Define it in one sentence, then state one thing your definition does not cover.

non_fit — definition-nonfit-01

Stimulus: That means everyone who disagrees is acting in bad faith.

Explain why this may be an interpretation or accusation rather than a definition. Rewrite it more carefully.

control — definition-control-01

Stimulus: The audience already shares the term’s ordinary meaning.

Explain why adding a definition may create unnecessary friction, and offer a concise transition instead.

3. candidate-call_to_action
recognition — action-recognition-01

Stimulus: By Friday, the project owner will publish the draft and invite comments.

Identify the action, owner, time horizon and remaining limit. Distinguish it from a broad aspiration.

transfer — action-transfer-01

Stimulus: Your team has identified one small problem it can address.

Write a call to action with an owner, resource, time horizon and consent or opt-out condition.

non_fit — action-nonfit-01

Stimulus: Everyone must share this immediately or they do not care.

Identify the coercive or unsafe features and rewrite the request with a bounded alternative.

control — action-control-01

Stimulus: You are closing a talk with appreciation but no action request.

Write a gratitude-based close that does not imply the audience owes an action.
