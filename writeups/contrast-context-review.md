contrast. Neighboring captions supply the opposition: inability to explain or

## What the result does and does not mean

All ten selected contrast candidates now have support under the broad rubric.
That 10/10 is not an independently measured classifier precision or a general
population estimate. The review deliberately reuses a selected legacy sample,
and its subtypes differ substantially. Some cases are monologues, reported words
or dialogue; uploader/video counts do not establish speaker independence.

For practice, a writer can identify the two ideas being opposed and make their
relation clear. That is a provisional drafting exercise, not evidence that a
particular voice, gesture or confrontational style improves persuasion. The
practice card now spells out these limits rather than calling all contrast
“disagreement handling.”
