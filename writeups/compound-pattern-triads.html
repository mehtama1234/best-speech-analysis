Three-move speech-pattern practice

These are corpus-mined triads that co-occurred in at least three reviews. Co-occurrence is not proof of order or effectiveness.

triad-audience_invitation_and_next_turn-then-contrast_and_reversal-then-objection_and_response

Make the next turn specific, answerable, and safe to decline. → then Make the earlier frame and the changed frame visible without a false binary. → then State the objection fairly, answer it, and preserve the remaining uncertainty.

Co-occurrence: 5 reviews

Draft a talk that tests these three candidate moves in sequence, explaining the bridge between each move.

Three-way co-occurrence is a discovery signal, not evidence of order, compatibility, causality or effectiveness.
