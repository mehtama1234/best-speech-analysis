At 327–363, three concepts are named and explained: self-reliance, securing oneself
and self-confidence. The first includes developing capabilities and a claim of

The closing (364–394) makes cooperation central again. An island/ocean metaphor

inventory of activities. The promised contribution remains a commitment, not a
