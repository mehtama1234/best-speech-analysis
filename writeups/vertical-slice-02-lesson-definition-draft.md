# Candidate lesson: define without overclaiming

Status: draft candidate lesson; not independently reviewed or learner-tested.

A definition can translate a word, reformulate a concept or explain an
implication. Before teaching a definition form, name which of those jobs is
being done. Then give a bounded meaning and say what remains outside it.

Practice: define one unfamiliar term literally, translate one culturally loaded
term, and reformulate one abstract idea as a practical consequence. Ask a
listener to restate each and identify what the definition did not claim.

Non-fit check: mark any sentence where “means” introduces interpretation,
analogy, reassurance or persuasion rather than a stable definition.
