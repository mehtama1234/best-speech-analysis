# Vertical slice 02: internal multimodal review

This internal review samples eight raw-media intervals from the three draft
packets. It records caption visibility, setting and edit boundaries only. It is
not independent validation and does not infer intent, audience response or
effectiveness.

| Candidate | Intervals | Exact caption alignment | Main limitation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Question | 3 | 2 visible, 1 edit/title frame | A question form does not establish turn ownership or response |
| Definition/reformulation | 2 | 1 visible, 1 nearby boundary transition | Translation and reformulation require adjacent context |
| Call to action | 3 | 2 visible, 1 setting-only frame | Imperative wording does not establish feasibility or uptake |

The authoritative observations are in
`research/vertical-slice-02-manual-multimodal-review.json`.
