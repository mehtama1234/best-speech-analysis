# Communication-job listener-outcome protocol

Status: **design complete, not run**.

A result can show differences in the measured task for the authored stimuli and tested population. It cannot establish that a celebrity's delivery or a speech form causes trust, long-term memory, persuasion, engagement or action generally.

Jobs: **18** · common listener tasks: **6** · stimulus pairs locked: **no**.

## Listener tasks

| Task | Measure |
|---|---|
| `main_point_recall` | blind rubric score 0–2 |
| `evidence_or_example_identification` | blind rubric score 0–2 |
| `limit_or_condition_identification` | blind rubric score 0–2 |
| `requested_action_or_role` | blind rubric score 0–2 |
| `confusion_and_load` | coded response plus optional rating |
| `delayed_recall` | blind rubric score 0–2; only when feasible and consented |

## Stimulus requirement

Each job requires a newly authored matched control/pattern-informed pair. Source celebrity identity and copied quotations are not experimental manipulations.

## Safety and accessibility

- Use plain-language consent and allow withdrawal without penalty.
- Offer text, captioned audio/video and screen-reader-compatible response modes.
- Do not require trauma, medical, political or personal disclosure.
- Minimize retention of recordings; obtain separate permission for recording.

## Not yet evidence

No matched stimulus pairs or consented listener observations are present.
