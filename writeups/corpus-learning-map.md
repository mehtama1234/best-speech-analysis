# Corpus learning map: start here

Status: **395-video transcript synthesis with provisional practice hypotheses; not validated best practice**.

This is the short route into the full corpus: **395 videos**, **4016 recorded function observations**, **2800 structural relations** and **50 exact phrase-form hypotheses**. The primary heuristic route gives 207 videos a provisional practice job; a separate named-relation route adds 28 candidate routes, leaving 160 without either route. Those rows should be read through their evidence, limits and next test rather than forced into a lesson.

The catalog records what captions and full-context writeups contain. It does not prove that a phrase works, that a delivery feature caused an effect, or that a speaker's story is factually true.

## The fastest way to use it

1. Pick the communication problem, not a famous speaker.
2. Read one exact example and its surrounding context.
3. Rewrite the underlying move in your own words.
4. Add the relevant evidence, condition, consent and limit.
5. Try it in an accessible format and ask one listener to restate it.
6. Keep the useful function, record the non-fit case and revise.

## How to inspect one video

Choose any row in the [full corpus findings catalog](corpus-findings-catalog.md). Follow its linked writeup, exact evidence IDs, observed functions, structural relations, boundary cases, limits and next test. The [unclassified lesson leads](unclassified-lesson-leads.md) give every remaining video a reviewable claim and next test without assigning a forced practice label.

For vocal, visual and accessibility practice, use the [delivery learning guide](delivery-learning-guide.md). The current delivery layer is partial: raw media availability, derived proxies and internal frame notes are not independent nonverbal findings.

## Claim ladder

`transcript observation` → `function hypothesis` → `recurrence candidate` → `delivery observation` → `mechanism hypothesis` → `teachability evidence` → `listener outcome`

The current map is strongest at the first two levels and provisional at the next three. Learner and listener tests remain required before calling any move a tested best practice.
