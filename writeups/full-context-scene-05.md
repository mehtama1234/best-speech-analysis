## Reassurance can be heard in the opposite way

At 56–66, a request to hear a joke meets reluctance and an expressed concern about
embarrassment. The requester promises not to laugh. In context, that can mean
not ridiculing the teller, but it can also mean the joke will fail to amuse.

The exchange distinguishes two kinds of resolution. One speaker clarifies what
was meant, but the joke is still withheld because expectations are now described
