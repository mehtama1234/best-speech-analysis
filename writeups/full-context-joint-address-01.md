## Candidate patterns and next checks

Candidates include reciprocal lesson ownership, a shared scene retold through
two perspectives, concrete motifs that return with changed functions, and a
summary that compresses a completed roadmap. Keep the partners' recollections
linked: multiple speakers in one coordinated address do not automatically supply
independent evidence for the advice.

A provisional exercise for a joint presentation is to give each speaker a
distinct contribution, show where the other's thinking changed an approach, and
make the shared conclusion explicit. Test whether listeners can identify both
the distinction and the connection. No practice card or audience-effect claim
is admitted by this reading.
