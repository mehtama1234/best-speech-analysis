## How to use a candidate

1. Open the source interval and listen across the entire captioned unit.
2. Decide whether the gap is speech, silence, editing, music or missing audio.
3. Record `indeterminate` when the source does not establish the cause.
4. Rehearse the wording with and without the timing feature and ask a consenting listener for restatement.
5. Keep the observation local to the wording, speaker and access mode; do not promote it to a general best practice.
