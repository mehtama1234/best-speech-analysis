## What holds the address together

The opening contrasts the prestigious graduation occasion with the speaker's
own non-graduation, then limits the promise to three personal stories (0–7).
This supplies a manageable roadmap without announcing an abstract theory.
The first story starts directly; unlike the second and third, it has no
separate thematic heading in this retained text. Familiar versions must not
be used to silently supply one.

The common thread is how to choose under uncertainty. But the three sections
do not use an identical story-first template. The first two interpret past
reversals after their outcomes; the third introduces a recurring decision
question before describing a medical encounter. Calling all three the same
formula would erase a useful structural distinction.

## First story: the answer takes a detour through the stakes

The dropout question at 10 does not receive an immediate financial answer.
Adoption and the parental promise of college come first (11–25). That detour
explains why leaving college breaks an important expectation. Expense and
uncertain purpose then explain the decision (26–37).

The text does not make this transition painless: fear, unstable accommodation
and food constraints remain alongside positive hindsight (33–44). The concrete
calligraphy example then connects apparently impractical learning to later
design work (45–65). Specific details make the proposed connection inspectable
as a story; they do not independently prove the corporate history or the
counterfactual that other computers would otherwise lack these features.

At 66–72, backward intelligibility becomes a forward-facing invitation to
trust. This is the critical inferential jump. A successful retrospective
example does not establish how often such choices work, which alternatives
failed, or whether a graduate should make the same educational decision.

## Second story: reject an institution without rejecting the work

Growth, a recent product and the founder's age heighten the reversal into
dismissal (73–88). Months of uncertainty and an attempted apology remain in
the account (89–93); the later lesson was not presented as immediately obvious.

The hinge is continuity: institutional rejection has not eliminated attachment
to the work (94–97). Later ventures, a return and family life support the
speaker's revised interpretation of dismissal (98–111). This parallels the
first story's positive hindsight, but here the mechanism is renewed capacity
to begin, not a previously unrelated skill finding an application.

The final instructions repeat searching and refusing to settle (112–125).
Work and romantic attachment are linked through an analogy. The stronger
claims that love is the only route to great work or satisfaction remain the
speaker's generalizations, not findings established by this case. Neither
rejection nor adversity is shown to be necessary for everyone else's growth.

## Third story: an abstract test becomes personally situated

The mortality question is introduced before the diagnosis story (126–142).
An important qualifier is easy to lose in a short inspirational excerpt:
change follows an unwanted answer over too many days, not every isolated bad
day (129–133). The address offers no operational threshold or decision-risk
assessment beyond that wording.

The medical account translates an initial prognosis into ordinary family
obligations (143–154). A later biopsy changes the reported diagnosis and is
followed by a historical health update (155–162). Keep both stages: quoting
the initial prognosis alone would misrepresent the retained story. The account
of clinicians' reaction is relayed through the wife while the speaker reports
being sedated; it is not a directly observed reaction in this analysis.

At 163–177, the speaker explicitly distinguishes an intellectual idea from
the greater certainty he attributes to experience. That is an epistemic claim
inside the speech, not proof of a universally correct decision method. The
generational metaphor then makes mortality relevant to the graduating audience.
The clinical narrative is not medical guidance or a current health statement.

## The ending converges instead of mechanically recapping

The closing instructions reunite limited time, resistance to external opinion,
heart and intuition (178–183). They connect earlier motifs without listing the
three stories again. A publication memory then supplies a tangible closing
artifact: a verbally described road photograph and an attributed farewell
motto (184–206).

The motto changes ownership and audience across its repetitions: publication
editors' farewell, the speaker's continuing wish for himself, then a wish for
graduates. These are distinct discourse functions, not three independent
examples of audience impact. The road image is described in words; no displayed
photograph, camera shot, gesture, pause or vocal emphasis has been verified.
