# Corpus learner-card inventory

Status: **transcript-derived practice navigation; not validated curriculum**.

Every card is a way to learn from and inspect a recorded transcript review. It does not establish that the move works, that the speaker intended the inferred function, or that the form should be used universally.

This inventory contains **4016 function cards** and **2800 sequence cards** from **395 full-context reviews**. Each card preserves the original function label and reason, up to five exact caption segments, a provisional shared-job form where available, a drill, self-check, non-fit warning, limits and next test.

Function cards with exact evidence: **4016** · sequence cards with exact evidence: **2795** of 2800 · cards with phrase handles: **2289** · sequence cards with phrase handles: **1812** · phrase handles: **8062** · cards with a shared speech-job template: **1473** · publisher/media boundary cards: **149** · unclassified and requiring review: **2394**.

Use the [searchable inventory](corpus-learner-card-inventory.html) to filter by video, raw label, canonical job, relation type or wording. Use the linked full writeup and source context before treating a card as a lesson.

## How to use one card

1. Read the exact evidence and the surrounding writeup.
2. State the communication problem in your own words.
3. Try the repeatable form in a low-stakes example.
4. Run the self-check and note where the form does not fit.
5. Keep delivery, audience effect and factual claims separate until they are independently checked.
