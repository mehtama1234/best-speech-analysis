# Job phrase transfer cards

Status: **provisional phrase transfer cards; not validated**.

These cards join transcript-derived job hypotheses to exact phrase retrieval candidates. They do not prove that a phrase performs the job, that the source speaker intended it, or that adapting it improves delivery or listener outcomes.

Use the phrase as a retrieval handle, not a script. Rewrite it for your relationship and situation, then add the evidence, condition, consent or limit required by the job.

## `answer_or_response`

**Listener problem:** Respond to the literal prompt while addressing the underlying need.

**Repeatable form:** Answer the literal point; state a useful frame if needed; give the bounded answer.

**Why it may help:** Hypothesis: naming the frame and then answering can reduce mismatch between what was asked and what the listener needs, but reframing can also evade the question.

**Practice:** Give a direct answer, then a frame-corrected answer; ask which one remains responsive.

**Self-check:** Can the listener identify both the direct answer and any justified reframing?
