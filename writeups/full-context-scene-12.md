## Candidate pattern card

**Pattern:** goal → condition → repeated action cue → larger frame.

**Plain idea:** say what must happen, make the condition visible, repeat the cue
when the decision changes, and then explain why the moment matters.

**Practice use:** useful for a short dramatic story or a decision explanation.

**Do not copy:** threats, coercion, danger, explosive action, or fictional
character behavior. The scene does not prove that repetition increases compliance
or that a larger frame makes a real audience agree.
