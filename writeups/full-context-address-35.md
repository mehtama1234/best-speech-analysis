## Candidate patterns and limits

Candidates include a framework mapped to responsible roles; an awareness-to-
implementation contrast; partial success followed by a remaining limitation;
and a local repair from hope to commitment followed by a qualified outcome.
This source also supplies a useful boundary case: repeated numbered cues do not
guarantee clean section boundaries, and strong assertions do not supply evidence.

A provisional communication exercise is to state the shared framework, name who
owns each part, and distinguish existing work, requested next actions and hoped-for
results. Test whether listeners can accurately identify responsibilities rather
than assuming repetition or forceful delivery improves understanding. This is not
an endorsement of the policy proposals, and no practice card is admitted.
