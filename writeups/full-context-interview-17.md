## Research contribution and next test

Candidate patterns include correcting a premise, answering a repeated specificity
question with a limit, distinguishing symbolic progress from practical effect,
and combining long-run optimism with near-term difficulty. Compare independent
interviews and code whether follow-up questions actually receive the requested
evidence. Do not mistake eloquent rationale for implemented policy.
