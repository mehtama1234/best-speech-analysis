## Research contribution

This source supplies several meaningful scope corrections: no guaranteed
performance outcome, planning retained within heart language, balance through
prioritization rather than maximizing everything, and individual suitability
attached to an anecdotal practice. It also demonstrates that a host's requested
list structure need not be completed exactly by the answer.

Future comparisons should test each qualification against the claim it actually
limits. A disclaimer about success does not validate a happiness guarantee;
an individual-suitability caveat does not validate a health mechanism. Keep
supporting context attached before building practice principles.
