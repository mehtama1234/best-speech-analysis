## Candidate comparisons and tests

Candidates include defining optimism through a personal example that retains
hardship, separating information about harm from harm itself, preserving reality
while legitimizing humor, and ending with a conditional bridge from interest to
action. Test whether listeners retain those conditions rather than remembering
only a positive claim about technology or a political joke.

Compare independent original events and counterexamples where awareness does not
produce action. No practice card, validated psychological benefit, current-policy
claim or measured audiovisual delivery feature is admitted from this reading.
