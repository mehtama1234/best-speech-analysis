## Candidate patterns and next checks

Candidates include unity with explicit disagreement, historical inheritance
qualified by renewal obligations, personal identity broadened into inclusion,
and a common rationale carried across several domains. Distinguish those structures
from unqualified claims that tradition alone ensures cooperation.

A provisional exercise is to state what is shared, acknowledge what differs,
and specify why prior success is insufficient without further work. Ask readers
to identify the qualification and the requested next action. This is a hypothesis
for testing, not an admitted best-practice card or endorsement of policy claims.
