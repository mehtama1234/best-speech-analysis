## Limits of the design

This is a discovery pilot, not a completed corpus analysis. Equal video selection differs from equal-time or equal-caption sampling. Within-video block probabilities differ with video length; short tail blocks are retained. The manifest records model-based inclusion probabilities for core blocks, not padding. A fixed pseudorandom seed provides reproducibility; no population rate is inferred here. Missing transcripts, duplicate uploads, repeated speakers, edits and unknown formats limit generalization. Prior-review overlap is disclosed, not filtered out or treated as a held-out test.
