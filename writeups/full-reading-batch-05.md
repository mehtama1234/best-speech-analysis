## 2. King excerpt: recurring wording performs several different jobs

The retained text moves through historical promise, continuing injustice,
present urgency, nonviolent discipline, future scenes, collective activity and
an inclusive projected culmination. It is not verified as the full original
address, and garbled wording is preserved rather than repaired from memory.

| Repeated textual frame | Local function | What changes across instances |
|---|---|---|
| Elapsed time | Diagnose an unfulfilled promise | Forms of exclusion and deprivation |
| Present moment | Mark urgency | Proposed transitions and demands |
| Future dream | Present an alternative future | Places, relationships and social conditions |
| Shared faith | Link hope to collective capacity | Activities people could undertake together |

connects to the inclusive close, while the geographic sequence uses a not-only-
there expansion rather than a flat list.

These are textual structures, not claims about how the voice rises, where the
speaker pauses or how the audience responds. No song lyrics are reproduced in
this report, and the text's reference to singing does not establish an inspected
musical performance.

## What this batch adds

Three distinctions should travel into later analysis:

- Editorial repetition is not another independent occurrence of a technique.
- A repeated verbal frame can change function as its content changes.
- A response may supply background, answer explicitly and then qualify its
  scope; those are not interchangeable with simply answering or evading.

These distinctions require careful provenance before practice recommendations.
Future media work should verify the preview duplication and demonstrative
actions, while the King source needs trustworthy alignment before any delivery
measurements. No legacy labels, practice cards or pause judgments change here.
