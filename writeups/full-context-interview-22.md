behavioral measure. Later acknowledgment of organizations that dislike the show

## Candidate tests, not admitted best practices

Compare value-first introductions with direct problem statements across independent
events, controlling for topic, format, celebrity status and audience familiarity.
Test what listeners understand and remember, including qualifications, rather
than assuming an emotional response improves comprehension. Seek cases where a
family-role frame narrows inclusion or obscures the broader rights issue. Compare
self-inclusion with direct accountability language without presuming one always
works better. Verify original program excerpts before coding delivery and obtain
independent outcome evidence before attributing impact. No practice card or
audiovisual finding is admitted by this reading.
