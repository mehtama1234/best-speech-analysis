## What these change in the research approach

Three context relationships deserve explicit tracking:

- **Scope:** which proposition a qualification applies to, and whose words it is.
- **Reversal:** whether a later rejection changes an earlier apparent request.
- **Retraction or disclosure:** whether the source admits claims were invented.

These are relationships across segments, not merely new labels on isolated
words. They reinforce the need to read episodes and retain source boundaries.
The existing candidate rules and provisional cards do not yet model all these
relationships; these reviews identify requirements, not a completed solution.

A proposed exercise is to mark the later sentence that changes an earlier
sentence's meaning, then explain the difference between the isolated excerpt
and the full exchange. For a success story, list what evidence would rule out
alternative explanations. These are critical-reading exercises, not validated
persuasion techniques.
