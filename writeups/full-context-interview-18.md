## What should be compared next

Candidate structures include the completed-chapter/new-challenge account,
reported alternatives used to explain a choice, host-supplied premises echoed
in answers, and a change from institutional ambitions to an operational role's
objectives. Compare promotional and adversarial interviews before calling any of
these general speech practices.

One test should ask whether readers can distinguish the question actually asked
from the broader argument supplied in response. Another should test recall of
conditions, such as coach approval, rather than only confident declarations.
Original-event deduplication and continuous media verification are still needed.
Applause labels, repeated self-descriptions and host praise do not establish
persuasion, confidence, vocal emphasis or visible expression. No practice card
or verified social/sporting outcome is admitted.
