## Address: a capacity/outcome contradiction becomes a collective appeal

India team and describes an opportunity for wider work. The opening establishes
belonging and obligation rather than launching directly into the problem.

At 36–51, inventive human capacity is contrasted with continued harm and unmet
needs. At 52–62, reported screen images make those categories concrete. The
examples carry emotional stakes in the argument, but their vividness is not a

The appeal at 63–76 asks listeners to join a collective voice and credits the

## Candidate patterns and next tests

concrete examples followed by collective appeal, and a maxim expanded through
parallel action clauses. For the panel, candidates include prompted elaboration,
implementation qualifications and acknowledged resistance within a change story.
