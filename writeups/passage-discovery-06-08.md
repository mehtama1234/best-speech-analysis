## Passage 6: a catalogue compressed into an image of loss

The first-person opening presents extraordinary visual recollections (0–5).
The passage then gathers those moments into a statement about their loss,
uses a brief rain-related simile, and closes with a mortality statement (5–11).
This gives a compact sequence to compare with longer experience-to-generalization
passages. It is fictional dialogue, not performer autobiography or proof of
the performer's state of mind.

Several proper-name-like phrases are badly garbled. They are not reconstructed
from knowledge of the film. The broad catalogue-to-loss structure is legible,
but exact lexical analysis should wait for source verification. Five subsequent
captions contain only music markers. They are retained records, not five spoken
sentences or independently detected musical events. Caption count and reliable
spoken-language coverage are therefore different denominators.

The transcript's short fragments do not measure pause duration, vocal emphasis,
facial expression or pacing. Those remain questions for localized media review.

## Passage 7: a historical account becomes a graduate appeal

A narrated mission and photograph are followed by an inventory of institutional
changes and a conclusion about perspective (81–104). This is a speaker's causal
account, not independent evidence that the photograph caused those changes.
The sweeping assertions about history and representation are unverified.

The following argument lists expense, danger and uncertain risk, then compares
public exploration with a maritime example (107–114). Its presence shows how
the speaker organizes reasons and analogy; it does not establish a universal
rule about government and private enterprise.

The address pivots to graduates. A metaphor for narrow disciplinary knowledge
contrasts with a list of disciplines involved in a larger undertaking. A
question-answer frame then reinterprets the diploma as an invitation to recognize
how much remains to learn (115–128). This is normative reframing, not a literal
definition of the credential. An asserted institutional legacy connects to a
closing appeal for graduates to lead (129–135).

The source's instruction to look something up is part of the speech, not an
instruction adopted by this review. Historical claims remain attributed rather
than verified or silently presented as present-day facts.

## Passage 8: acknowledgment organized through relationships

Thanks move through a collaborator, venue and organization (0–21). A longer
set of acknowledgments leads into repeated dependence statements about the
people whose support made the occasion possible (22–49).

A career-origin account then connects a move, ambition, manager, agents, family
and a training setting (50–89). This is an opportunity chain as narrated by
the speaker, not independently established biography or causal proof. The close
returns to friends, family and films, then invites the next ceremony activity
(90–109). Reported parental advice differs from the speaker's present request.

Repeated thanks serve acknowledgment rather than uncertainty. Questions about
where named people are appear to locate addressees in the text, not necessarily
invite reflection on an argument. The final invitation is procedural within
the ceremony; it is not automatically a general call for life change.

## Comparisons worth testing

These passages qualify a broad experience-to-claim hypothesis. The ceremony
address repeatedly distributes credit rather than always using experience to
prove a collective proposition. The fictional monologue compresses recollections
into a closing image without an audience action request. The graduation speech
does lead to an explicit appeal, but its causal account requires evidence beyond
the order in which events are narrated.

Repeated wording, questions and lists therefore need a function label in context:
acknowledgment, reframing, description, argument or procedure. Their frequency
alone does not demonstrate a best practice. Genre and preparation are important
comparison variables, not nuisance details to pool away.

A proposed exercise is to trace each ending back to the material it gathers:
moments, institutional history or relationships. Then ask whether the closing
claim follows, changes the subject, or simply performs a ceremonial function.
This is an analysis exercise, not a tested recommendation for audience impact.
