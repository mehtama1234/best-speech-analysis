# Candidate lesson: make the next step accountable

Status: draft candidate lesson; not independently reviewed or learner-tested.

“We need to” and “let us” can make direction visible, but imperative wording is
not the same as a feasible or ethical plan. Name the action, owner, resources,
time horizon, consent condition and limit. Scale the request to the speaker's
actual authority.

Practice: rewrite one broad appeal as a personal, team and institutional action.
For each version, state who can act, by when, with what resource and what the
request cannot solve.

Non-fit check: distinguish a genuine next step from a closing aspiration, a
conversation transition or advice quoted from someone else.
