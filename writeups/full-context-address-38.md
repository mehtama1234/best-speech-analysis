## Candidate patterns and next tests

Candidates include story-first concept introduction, analogy reused across
domains, a framework revisited in a different order, and a later example that
limits an earlier metaphor. The self-implicating close turns criticism toward
the speaker before handing responsibility to listeners. None establishes
audience comprehension, persuasion or actual listening behavior.

A provisional exercise is to define a concept, show more than one application,
and include a case where the same wording would not fit the listener. Ask readers
to identify that limit. Test multi-label treatment where examples overlap rather
than assuming a numbered list creates exclusive categories. No practice card is
admitted by this reading.

Next verify uncertain source wording and original media, compare metaphor limits
and reordered summaries across distinct events, and inspect continuous audio/video
before claims about silence, pacing, gaze or emotion. Full retained-text coverage
adds contextual evidence, not clinical validation or delivery measurements.
