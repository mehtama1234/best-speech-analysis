Listener outcome record

Protocol boundary: This form records comprehension/recall observations for a predefined stimulus. It does not measure trust, persuasion, liking or behavior, and it does not deliver or store recordings. Do not use it until stimuli, condition assignment and consent procedures are locked.
Session
main_point_recall

State the speaker's main point in your own words without seeing the script.

evidence_or_example_identification

Name the example, reason or evidence the speaker used, if any.

limit_or_uncertainty_identification

State one limit, uncertainty or condition the speaker included, or say that none was clear.

requested_action_identification

What action, if any, did the speaker invite? Include owner or condition if stated.

confusion_and_load

Report what was confusing, rushed, emotionally difficult or easy to follow; do not require agreement.

delayed_recall

After a defined delay, restate the main point and one supporting detail.
