## What this contributes to the research

This source supplies candidate examples of role-based counterquestions, a shift
from private conflict to public address, repeated evaluative questions, and moral
category reversal. These mechanisms are analytically useful even when the conduct
is destructive. They must remain in a scripted-scene stratum, with writing,
performance and editing recognized as possible contributors. Multiple lines in
this clip are not independent cross-speaker replications.

The next useful comparison would ask whether audiences retain the original
criticism after a vivid counteraccusation, distinguishing comprehension, memorability,
approval and actual persuasion. Include scenes where the redirection fails or is
explicitly challenged. Before adding voice or gesture claims, inspect the original
clip, resolve speaker turns and verify synchronization. No practice card, behavioral
diagnosis or claim of real-world effectiveness is admitted by this analysis.
